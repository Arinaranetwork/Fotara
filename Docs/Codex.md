# Codex - Fotara

## What Is This
Fotara is a specialized Android application designed to organize photos of notes, assignments, whiteboards, and study material into clearly labeled, searchable folders. By combining a folder-based subject hierarchy with on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Fotara makes handwritten and photographed text fully searchable, eliminating the chaos of flat, unstructured camera rolls.

## Who Is It For
College and high school students who frequently take reference photos of coursework, lecture slides, assignments, and study materials across multiple concurrent subjects and need immediate filing and fast keyword retrieval.

## How to Use
1. Open Fotara to view subject folder cards (e.g., Biology, Calculus, History) arranged on the dark midnight dashboard.
2. Tap "+New" to create a new subject folder, select a distinctive color label, and optionally pin it to the top.
3. Tap into any folder to view subfolder tabs (such as All, Lectures, Assignments) and browse organized photo notes.
4. Capture or import a photo directly into a folder or subfolder. Fotara straightens documents and automatically indexes handwritten and printed text in the background.
5. Use the search bar at the top of the home screen to find any note instantly by keywords written on the page, folder name, or attached deadline.

## Constraints & Assumptions
- **Platform**: Android (minSdk 26 Android 8.0 Oreo, targetSdk 36).
- **Offline First**: All storage, indexing, OCR, and search must operate entirely on-device without external server dependencies or cloud subscription requirements.
- **Hardware**: Device camera and local storage permissions required.
- **Out of Scope for Initial Version**: Cloud synchronization across multiple devices, collaborative folder sharing, and desktop clients.
