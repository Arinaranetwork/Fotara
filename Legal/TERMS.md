## 1. What Is Fotara
Fotara is an offline-first study organization and coursework notebook application designed for Android devices. The application helps students, researchers, and self-learners manage subject folders, capture whiteboard photos, annotate lecture slides, write rich text and markdown notes, view documents, and schedule assignment deadline alerts.
